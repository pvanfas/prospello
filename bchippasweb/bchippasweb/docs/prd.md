# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - Bichippas Brand Website

**Project**: Bichippas Brand Website  
**Client**: Bichippas Food products  
**Location**: Areekode  
**Primary Audience**: Malayali Audience  

## Overview
Bichippas is a packed food product brand based in Kerala. The objective of this website is brand elevation and product showcasing. The website will focus on storytelling, strong brand identity, and clear presentation of products rather than direct ecommerce.

## Project Goals
- Strengthen Bichippas brand identity
- Visually showcase products in a modern and engaging way
- Communicate the brand journey and values
- Enable B2B enquiries
- Provide clear product information and usage ideas

## Core Modules and Features

### 1. Home Page
**1.1 Landing Page**
- Modern hero section with a carousel
- Carousel displays product images, short descriptions, and brand identity elements
- Focus on visual impact and brand recall

**1.2 Brand Story Section**
- Storytelling section with a scroll based parallax effect
- Timeline based narrative showing the brand journey from inception to present
- Emphasis on growth, milestones, and values

**1.3 Product Highlights Section**
- Display selected key products with strong brand visuals
- Each product card includes: Product image, Product name, Short description, Know More button
- View All Products button redirects to the products listing page

**1.4 B2B Enquiry Form**
- Dedicated enquiry section for business partners
- Form fields include: Name, Email, Phone number, Message
- Send button to submit the enquiry

**1.5 Contact and Call to Action Section**
- Clear CTA encouraging users to get in touch
- Contact details or quick enquiry prompts

### 2. Product Listing Page
**2.1 Products Listing Page**
- Displays all products under categorized sections
- Each product card includes: Thumbnail image, Product name, Product type or category, Know More button

### 3. Product Details Page
**3.1 Product Detail Page**
- Detailed product view including: Multiple product images, Product name, Detailed description
- Recipes section: List of recipes that can be prepared using the product
- Clicking on a recipe opens step by step preparation details

## Design and UX Guidelines
- Clean, modern, and premium design
- Strong focus on brand colors and identity
- High quality product imagery
- Smooth animations and subtle transitions
- Mobile responsive and performance optimized
